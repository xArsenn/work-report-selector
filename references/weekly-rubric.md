# Weekly work report rubric

Use this rubric to predict likely 优秀周报 selection and to improve one weekly report. It is inferred from confirmed winners, not a confirmed company policy.

## Score dimensions (100 points)

### 1. Weekly core and milestone closure — 25

- 22–25: One or two material weekly transformations reached a clear delivered, launched, validated, settled, or decision-ready state.
- 16–21: Strong milestones were completed, with bounded validation or rollout remaining.
- 8–15: Several useful outputs exist, but the week's central accomplishment is diffuse or mostly intermediate.
- 0–7: A chronological activity list with little accumulated result.

### 2. Evidence and measurable change — 20

- 17–20: Coherent before/after, cumulative scope, quality, finance, conversion, reliability, or acceptance evidence.
- 12–16: Several meaningful counts or one strong comparison that supports the result.
- 6–11: Specific work is named but impact or scale remains hard to verify.
- 0–5: Mostly qualitative claims or decorative numbers.

Prefer complete chains such as spend → leads → acquisition cost → orders → revenue, or latency → throughput/errors → resource use → production verification.

### 3. Business value and strategic relevance — 20

- 17–20: Direct material effect on revenue, cash, cost, acquisition, delivery, customer experience, capacity, data accuracy, compliance, security, or continuity.
- 12–16: Clear operational value tied to a team or product objective.
- 6–11: Local usefulness with weak linkage to an organizational priority.
- 0–5: The reader cannot tell why the week mattered.

### 4. Problem solving, quality, and risk control — 15

- 13–15: Root cause and fix are paired with regression, reconciliation, canary, capacity, fallback, security, or other strong verification.
- 9–12: A meaningful problem was solved and partly verified.
- 4–8: Routine fixes or unresolved diagnosis with a plausible direction.
- 0–3: Issues are listed without ownership or response.

### 5. Systemization and reusable assets — 10

- 9–10: The week produced reusable automation, SOP, test set, monitoring, fallback, checklist, protocol, platform capability, or durable data definition.
- 6–8: Reusable design or documentation exists but adoption or validation is incomplete.
- 3–5: Some repeatability, mostly one-off execution.
- 0–2: No durable output beyond isolated activity.

### 6. Reflection and next-week continuity — 10

- 9–10: Honest review plus next steps directly derived from current risks or unfinished milestones, with concrete objects and acceptance conditions.
- 6–8: Specific next-week actions or useful lessons, but continuity is partial.
- 3–5: Generic reflection or `继续完善` plans.
- 0–2: No meaningful follow-through.

A separate reflection or next-week section is optional if the same information is embedded clearly in the outcome paragraphs.

## Deductions

Apply conservatively and avoid double-counting:

- −3 to −8: Many tasks but no weekly priority or accumulated outcome.
- −2 to −6: Numbers are plentiful but do not support a decision or claimed result.
- −3 to −10: Development or proposal is described as launched, accepted, or effective without evidence.
- −2 to −6: Major unfinished work is hidden rather than bounded with its next action.
- −2 to −5: Training, meetings, or AI-tool usage is presented as the achievement without application or output.

## Interpretation

- 85–100: Strong winner
- 75–84: Likely shortlist / winner depending on quota
- 65–74: Competitive weekly report that can win depending on the comparison set
- 50–64: Useful work, but the weekly narrative or evidence is underdeveloped
- Below 50: Unlikely to be selected

Rank comparable weekly periods against one another. Do not compare raw totals across job families; compare outcome strength relative to role, scope, and controllability.
