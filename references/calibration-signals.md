# Calibration signals from confirmed winners

Use these observations as company-specific positive signals. They come from confirmed selected reports, but the absence of non-winning controls means they do not prove an exact threshold or causal algorithm.

## Confirmed positive patterns

### Finance and operational-control work

A confirmed winner combined several moderate but concrete finance outcomes:

- A reimbursement-policy revision with explicit timeliness and no-invoice compliance improvements.
- Bank query-account setup that was verified to support automatic transaction collection and reduce manual work.
- Vendor renewal progress with pricing details understood, an intended negotiation, and a mid-month completion target.
- A demonstrated internal finance AI tool plus a credible next discussion forum.

Implication: a report does not need one spectacular number when it combines compliance, automation, cost awareness, verified enablement, and dated follow-through. Score the portfolio of outcomes, not only the largest item.

### Content-production work

A confirmed winner reported 60 Xiaohongshu image posts made from 6 templates, 4 vertical videos, supporting material organization, and a concise result recap. It connected ChatGPT use to workflow optimization rather than treating the tool mention as the achievement.

Implication: for roles whose direct deliverable is production volume, completed unit counts are meaningful evidence. Template reuse, format variety, material readiness, and process improvement strengthen the signal even when conversion data is not yet available. A short `今日成果` recap that repeats the decisive numbers can help machine extraction.

### Store operations and collections

A confirmed winner reported 12 stores processed, 5 same-day collections, and 42% progress, while separating completed tasks from items requiring continued monitoring. It also included system migration, performance planning, ad-account setup, and an automated WeCom reply setup.

Implication: full completion is not mandatory. Partial results can be strong when denominator, completed count, progress rate, pending condition, and next monitoring action are explicit. Multiple workstreams also score well when each has a clear state and at least one is tied directly to cash collection or operating performance.

## Calibration adjustments

- Treat 65–74 as a genuinely competitive band, not merely an average report; confirmed winners can occur here depending on the day's field and quota.
- Reward denominator + achieved count + progress rate more than a percentage alone.
- Reward honest `未消耗/持续关注` status when paired with completed setup and a concrete monitoring action.
- Do not require a separate reflection or tomorrow-plan section when the body already contains clear next steps.
- Do not infer that mentioning ChatGPT or AI is itself favored. The confirmed winners connect AI to output, process improvement, demonstration, or future adoption.

## Remaining uncertainty

Do not infer a stable cutoff, department quota, or exact weight from winners alone. Request non-winning reports from the same dates and job families when the user wants stronger calibration.
