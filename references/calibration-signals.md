# Calibration signals from confirmed winners

Use these observations as company-specific positive signals. They come from confirmed selected reports, but the absence of non-winning controls means they do not prove an exact threshold or causal algorithm.

## Confirmed leadership rule

The company owner explicitly requires daily reports not to be too long. The exact limit is not yet known. Treat concise delivery as a real selection constraint: retain outcomes, numbers, exact states, and next actions; remove repetition, background narration, decorative language, and unrealized-value explanations first. Do not infer a character count until one is confirmed.

## Confirmed positive patterns

### Finance and operational-control work — daily positive pattern

A confirmed winner combined several moderate but concrete finance outcomes:

- A reimbursement-policy revision with explicit timeliness and no-invoice compliance improvements.
- Bank query-account setup that was verified to support automatic transaction collection and reduce manual work.
- Vendor renewal progress with pricing details understood, an intended negotiation, and a mid-month completion target.
- A demonstrated internal finance AI tool plus a credible next discussion forum.

Implication: in a daily-report context, a report does not need one spectacular number when it combines compliance, automation, cost awareness, verified enablement, and dated follow-through. Do not assume the same portfolio is sufficient for a weekly winner without cumulative change, stronger closure, or adoption evidence.

### Content-production work

A confirmed winner reported 60 Xiaohongshu image posts made from 6 templates, 4 vertical videos, supporting material organization, and a concise result recap. It connected ChatGPT use to workflow optimization rather than treating the tool mention as the achievement.

Implication: for roles whose direct deliverable is production volume, completed unit counts are meaningful evidence. Template reuse, format variety, material readiness, and process improvement strengthen the signal even when conversion data is not yet available. A short `今日成果` recap that repeats the decisive numbers can help machine extraction.

### Store operations and collections

A confirmed winner reported 12 stores processed, 5 same-day collections, and 42% progress, while separating completed tasks from items requiring continued monitoring. It also included system migration, performance planning, ad-account setup, and an automated WeCom reply setup.

Implication: full completion is not mandatory. Partial results can be strong when denominator, completed count, progress rate, pending condition, and next monitoring action are explicit. Multiple workstreams also score well when each has a clear state and at least one is tied directly to cash collection or operating performance.

## Calibration adjustments

- Treat 65–74 as a genuinely competitive band, not merely an average report; confirmed winners can occur here depending on the day's field and quota.
- Reward denominator + achieved count + progress rate more than a percentage alone.
- Reward honest `未消耗/持续关注` status when paired with completed setup and a concrete monitoring action.
- Do not require a separate reflection or tomorrow-plan section when the body already contains clear next steps.
- Do not infer that mentioning ChatGPT or AI is itself favored. The confirmed winners connect AI to output, process improvement, demonstration, or future adoption.

## Remaining uncertainty

Do not infer a stable cutoff, department quota, or exact weight from winners alone. Request non-winning reports from the same dates and job families when the user wants stronger calibration.

## Confirmed daily-report patterns — 2026-09-05 winners after administrator rule change

Three confirmed winners covered customer acquisition, communications operations, and store operations. Because only winners were supplied, treat these as new positive signals rather than a proven decision boundary.

### Measured execution plus honest outcome status

One acquisition report separated manual calls, AI outbound calls, and map-sourced leads with explicit counts: 30 manually contacted targets, 200 assigned AI-call targets with 100 actual calls, 1 preliminary-intent customer, and 40 newly sourced targets. It openly stated that manual calls produced no clear intent and that the AI lead still required human follow-up.

Implication: activity counts can support selection when they form a funnel and preserve honest outcomes. Reward planned-versus-actual execution, channel comparison, preliminary conversion signals, and a concrete validation step. Do not upgrade preliminary intent into conversion or treat AI usage itself as value.

### Routine safeguards become competitive when tied to risk closure

One communications-operations report confirmed that lines and SMS were checked with no anomaly, coordinated verification-code signature switching across carriers, audited monthly number/card activation risk, identified a small residual group that had not switched, and supplied local landlines as a fallback.

Implication: “no anomaly” is meaningful when it results from a named control check. Reward risk inventory, identification of the exposed group, and a completed fallback or mitigation even when no revenue metric exists. Named systems, carriers, affected scope, and mitigation state make operational-control work auditable.

### Multi-workstream delivery with a standout closed-loop project

One store-operations report combined a completed monthly performance-improvement plan, multi-supplier inventory inspection and allocation, script optimization, member follow-up issue handling across three cities, and progress on a Zhuhai performance project. The standout chain included completed development, launch and testing of AI automatic distribution, 8 real-time distributions with 1 failure, automated WeCom detail pushes, and a review identifying lower duplication as the next system-level objective.

Implication: a broad task portfolio can win when several items have clear completed states and one central project shows development → launch → test → measured operation → failure disclosure → next optimization. Reward concrete operating data and systemization; do not confuse an input count of 1 or an unresolved failure with full business validation.

### Likely direction of the changed preference

- High-confidence inference: the revised selection favors factual density—specific quantities, named objects, and explicit status—across very different job families.
- High-confidence inference: honest partial or negative results do not prevent selection when the report shows controlled execution and a concrete follow-up or mitigation.
- Medium-confidence inference: the administrator may be giving more credit to process validation, risk control, and reusable operating mechanisms, not only direct revenue or final conversion.
- Tentative signal: concise reports with three clear workstreams may be easier to select, but these samples do not establish a preferred length or item count.

### Confirmed non-winner calibration — finance daily report following the same rules

A finance daily report was confirmed not selected after being rewritten around a `今日核心`, four outcome sections, specific invoice-limit amounts, business-value explanations, and a next-day plan. Its strongest completed item was agreement on a business-data extraction definition, while the downstream July report had not yet been issued.

The other three items remained intermediate: invoice-limit increases of 100,000 and 200,000 yuan were applications under official review; contract-number management had a solution and initial bot configuration but still required completion; and payroll discrepancies were only partly located, with reconciliation and handling deferred to September. Claims about improved accuracy, safeguarded invoicing, smoother customer cooperation, and standardized management were prospective rather than measured same-day effects.

Calibration implications:

- A polished `今日核心 + 工作成果 + 明日计划` format does not create selection value by itself.
- Amounts attached to pending applications establish scope, not realized business impact.
- Agreement on a data definition is useful, but without an issued report, reconciliation result, acceptance, or before/after accuracy evidence it remains a partial milestone.
- A solution or initial configuration is not equivalent to a process being available and used.
- Partial root-cause discovery without the affected scope, corrected count, reconciliation result, mitigation, or closure remains diagnosis rather than problem closure.
- Future-value language should explain relevance only; it must not raise the score as though the benefit already occurred.
- Compared with the confirmed 2026-09-05 daily winners, this report lacks either a measured execution funnel, a completed fallback, or an operating system with live test data. This is a stronger separator than prose quality or section structure.
- This single negative sample supports a stricter daily closure gate for finance work, but does not prove a universal quota, cutoff, or ban on intermediate work.

### Confirmed non-winner batch — finance and administration daily reports

Three additional daily reports were confirmed not selected.

The first reported 11 store refund, member-recharge, and payment vouchers reviewed and prepared; bank-stamp materials for two stores were resubmitted and verified at the bank but still required follow-up; several other items were named without scale or outcome. The report was concise, showing that short length alone is insufficient when most content is routine or under-evidenced.

The second reported 27 vouchers processed, bookkeeping materials organized, and August bank-flow classification for an entire company group. Despite clear completion language and higher volume, the work remained routine throughput with no exception, accuracy result, elapsed-time improvement, reconciliation result, or other standout effect. This confirms that larger raw counts are not automatically favored.

The third reported bank-flow and tax-filing differences across seven companies, several employee departures, seal collection, six support tasks, attendance reconciliation, and an AI payroll-rule check. However, the financial differences were escalated through a project request rather than corrected; the AI issue received a new instruction but no verified rerun result; several tasks were routine administration; and the full report was duplicated verbatim, materially violating the confirmed brevity preference.

Calibration implications:

- Concision is a required constraint, not a sufficient reason for selection.
- Routine throughput counts such as 11 or 27 processed items show workload but remain weak selection evidence without exception handling, verified accuracy, efficiency change, unusual scope, or downstream effect.
- Broad administrative coverage can lower distinctiveness when no single item becomes a dominant closed result.
- `发现差异 + 提交立项`, or `发现规则问题 + 提交新指令`, remains diagnosis/escalation until correction and verification occur.
- Naming many companies or employees establishes scope but does not prove impact by itself.
- Full-text duplication is a strong negative signal because it directly violates the confirmed length constraint and sharply reduces information density.
- Across this batch and the prior finance non-winner, finance/admin reports appear to need more than routine completion: a corrected discrepancy, reconciled amount, verified automation, prevented operational interruption, completed fallback, or measured efficiency change is a stronger separator.
- Possible role or department effects remain tentative because the same-day full candidate pool is unavailable. Do not conclude that finance/admin work is categorically disadvantaged.

## Confirmed weekly-report patterns

### Confirmed non-winner calibration — finance report, 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-05

A finance weekly report was confirmed not selected despite a polished structure, a one-sentence summary, six named workstreams, setup of six company query accounts, one verified automatic bank-flow collection path, a one-click invoicing-tool upgrade, unified business-data definitions, invoice-limit applications, and risk discovery around bank-enterprise connectivity.

The report also contained several intermediate states: the reimbursement 2.0 proposal awaited confirmation and publication; July reporting under the new definition had not yet been produced; salary discrepancies were found but not cleared; invoice-limit requests remained under approval; and the bank-account cancellation issue was paused pending a decision. The AI finance tool was demonstrated, but actual adoption, usage frequency, time saved, or acceptance was not supplied.

Calibration implications:

- A clean summary and numerous named deliverables do not compensate for a missing dominant weekly outcome.
- Count `方案完成但待发布`, `申请完成但审批中`, `排查发现但待处理`, and `暂缓待确认` as intermediate progress rather than closure.
- Bank-account setup and one-path verification are useful evidence, but do not infer broad efficiency gains without coverage, before/after workload, saved time, or stable-use evidence.
- “One-click automation” describes capability; a strong weekly claim also needs actual use, acceptance, repeatability, or measured manual reduction when available.
- A list of unrelated finance improvements may read as accumulated daily tasks. Weekly selection appears to demand clearer concentration around one transformation plus cumulative or verified business change.
- This single non-winner corrects prior optimism about portfolio breadth in finance weekly reports, but it does not establish which individual feature caused rejection; the comparison pool and quota remain unknown.

These patterns recur across confirmed selected weekly reports from product, engineering, legal, operations, and growth roles.

### A weekly core compresses the period

Strong reports often begin with one sentence naming the week's main transformation: prototype to reviewed solution, manual to automated flow, single-point test to batch operation, unstable service to verified capacity, or platform activity to business-data closure. A formal one-line summary is helpful but not mandatory when the first result already performs this function.

### Before/after and cumulative scope are unusually strong

Recurring evidence includes latency before/after, throughput, error rate, resource reduction, cumulative cases, number of interfaces/services/channels/versions/contracts, spend, leads, acquisition cost, orders, revenue, and wall-clock savings. Reward a coherent measurement chain more than disconnected numbers.

### Verification protects the claimed result

Selected reports frequently state production canary validation, regression pass counts, byte-level reconciliation, capacity testing, zero-error behavior, rollout state, cross-source reconciliation, or explicit pending acceptance. This shows that the result is reliable rather than merely developed.

### Systemization outranks isolated fixes

High-value weekly work often leaves a reusable capability: an SOP, fallback path, automated diagnostic chain, permanent regression set, comparison table, deployment checklist, monitoring rule, data definition, account-management platform, or repeatable workflow. Reward the resulting organizational asset in addition to the immediate fix.

### Business and risk chains vary by role

- Product: user-visible behavior, decision alignment, rollout, versions, expiry readiness, and operational clarity.
- Engineering: bottleneck cause, measurable improvement, production validation, stability, capacity, security, and recovery.
- Legal: completed document volume, case-specific states, account unfreezing or response readiness, and prevention insight.
- Growth/operations: spend → opening/lead → acquisition → order → revenue, plus data-definition accuracy and scalable execution.

Do not require all roles to produce revenue. Compliance, avoided disruption, data accuracy, delivery readiness, and reduced manual work are valid value paths.

### Honest incompleteness can coexist with selection

Selected reports sometimes state that a direction remains unsettled, a chain still needs regression, or named tasks were not started. This is not automatically negative when the main weekly outcomes are substantial and pending work has a clear next step. Preserve these distinctions.

### Weekly continuity matters

Reward evidence that the report delivered a prior-week commitment and that next-week plans follow directly from current validation gaps, risks, or unfinished milestones. Generic plans such as `继续完善` are weak unless the object and acceptance condition are clear.
